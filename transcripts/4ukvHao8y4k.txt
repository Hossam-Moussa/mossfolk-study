But ditching resource cards comes&nbsp;&nbsp; with its own perils; the slow ramping of power done&nbsp; in a self-tracking way with nice hard reminders of&nbsp;&nbsp; how they work helps keep a balanced back and forth&nbsp; between players and by removing these cards, a new&nbsp;&nbsp; form of moderation is needed or else a game can&nbsp; dissolve into who is better at playing solitaire&nbsp;on turn one.
