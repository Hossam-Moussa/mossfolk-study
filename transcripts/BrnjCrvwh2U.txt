To fix this, small additions have been made to the core gameplay loops, bringing depth back to the game.    The simplest addition is various ships and equipment.  Each side, the Empire and the New Republic, has four ships to choose from.  The Empire has classic TIE models and the 
[4m] new Reaper ship.  And the New Republic has classic Rebel ships plus a U-wing addition.  In addition to this, ships can be modified with various equipment options. Equipment allows players to customize the components of a ship to better adapt it to certain situations.  The hull, shields, lasers, engine, and various auxiliary components are replaceable.  To make the gameplay more interesting, the ships also have an energy redirection system, like in the best science fiction.  The player chooses: to balance the ship's energy between shields, engines, and lasers, or to direct more energy into the selected subsystem at the expense of the other two. To most effectively solve problems, the player will often switch between these subsystems, making the gameplay a constant balancing act between ship integrity, objective fulfillment, and proper energy distribution. Of course, redirecting energy to different systems has its advantages.  More energy in lasers speeds up their recharge.  The energy in the engines makes the ship faster and more maneuverable, and in the 
[5m] shields—allows them to recharge faster and begin recovery without delay.  In addition to these efficiency advantages, each system has its own peculiarity. Fully charged lasers deal more damage. Fully charged shields accumulate additional protection above the basic one, and engines accumulate an acceleration charge, allowing for very high speeds.
