Level scaling is a relatively new term for the feature where enemies in a game world grow stronger in parallel with the player. A system to deliver dynamic difficulty no matter where the players wander is a great idea in theory. But in practice, level scaling can cause a lot of problems of its own. Allowing players to access everything right off the bat isn't really what we should be aiming for because an open world doesn't need to just feel open, it also needs to feel like a world.
