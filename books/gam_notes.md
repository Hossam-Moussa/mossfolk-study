# Chou notes (incremental, lossless)

## Ch1-3
- Sunk Cost Prison (black hat): his Diablo II quit story. Life as the game: "how to make games more meaningful / life more fun".
- Coonradt (The Game of Work, 1984) — 5 reasons hobbies beat work: clear goals, better scorekeeping/scorecards, more frequent feedback, higher personal choice of methods, consistent coaching.

- PBL Fallacy (Ch2): points/badges/leaderboards = shell. Social-media-experts analogy. "Learn from the design; don't copy the shell" (Trojan Horse). Grinding=grunt work made desirable by the WHY (level up, +5 strength). Secondhand sushi: must play games yourself to design.
- Good designer starts from FEELINGS (how do I want users to feel) → then picks elements. Bad designer starts from elements.
- Octalysis: 8 Core Drives (octagon position = nature of motivation):
  CD1 Epic Meaning & Calling (Wikipedia; Beginner's Luck)
  CD2 Development & Accomplishment (easiest to design; where PBLs live; badge w/o challenge meaningless)
  CD3 Empowerment of Creativity & Feedback (Legos; Evergreen Mechanics — self-refreshing, no new content needed)
  CD4 Ownership & Possession (virtual goods, customize avatar→more ownership; also process/org ownership)
  CD5 Social Influence & Relatedness (mentorship, acceptance, feedback, companionship, competition, envy; nostalgia)
  CD6 Scarcity & Impatience (Appointment Dynamics, Torture Breaks; Facebook exclusivity launch)
  CD7 Unpredictability & Curiosity (gambling, sweepstakes, novels; Skinner box = THIS drive only, not PBL)
  CD8 Loss & Avoidance (sunk cost, fading opportunities, "limited time only")
  + hidden CD9: Sensation (physical pleasure; excluded, psychological focus).
- Left Brain (left side) = extrinsic tendency: CD2,4,6 (logic, ownership, scarcity... he counts 2/4/6 left). Right Brain = intrinsic: CD3,5,7. Extrinsic rewards impair intrinsic (overjustification effect, Ch13). Better to design right-brain so "motivation sticks".
- White Hat = top (CD1,2,3): feel good/powerful, no urgency. Black Hat = bottom (CD6,7,8): urgency/obsession/addiction but feel bad; users leave when they can (Zynga "data-driven design" case; gambling). Black hat not inherently evil — can self-impose for gym/diet.
- Facebook octalysis: weak CD1+CD6, right-brain + black-hat-leaning (daily obsession), CD4 strong (collect/customize). Farmville lacks CD1+CD7 (no surprises). LinkedIn: left-brain white-hat (career)→profile sits idle; needs CD3+CD7; uses Social Prods & Social Treasures (ch9).
- Level II Octalysis: 4 Experience Phases: Discovery (why try), Onboarding (learn rules/tools), Scaffolding (regular journey of repeated actions toward goal), Endgame (retain veterans). Typical: Discovery←CD7, Onboarding←CD2, Scaffolding←CD5+CD6, Endgame←CD8. If no CD at any phase → dropout at that phase.
- Level III: × player types (Bartle A/S/E/K — Bartle himself doubts fit for gamification; can use any segmentation). 5 levels total; Level I enough for most. Games with impeccable Endgame design last decades/centuries.

## Ch12 CD8 Loss & Avoidance (cont.)
- Use CD8 ONLY at critical bumps where the Desired Action must happen NOW, then follow with White Hat drives to rebalance. Octalysis designs both motivation AND its nature (short vs long term).
- Poker all-in anatomy: rational brain concludes "he's not bluffing, I've lost" but emotional brain chases the 40% already in the pot (+CD5 pride: not looking like a loser, +CD7: "if I fold I'll NEVER KNOW his hand"). "Tough Muck" = folding a strong hand after heavy betting — mark of a professional. Second-largest hand is the worst hand at the table.
- Sunk cost in investing: $400M more into LOSR to "save" the sunk $1B; good money chasing bad; compelled but uncomfortable (Black Hat signature).
- Zombies, Run!: PLAYFUL CD8 — run because zombies chase you (vs Nike+ = CD2); plus CD1 (save Abel town), CD2 (missions), CD4 (collect resources to rebuild), CD7 (plot cliffhangers — companion's cough). Small doses of Black Hat = thrill, not burnout.
- CD8 often = other drives REVERSED (lose the meaning CD1, look like a loser CD5) — but must be designed as its OWN drive: loss-prevention ≈ 2x stronger than gain-seeking (Kahneman loss aversion; we take risks only when gain ≈ 2x potential loss).
- Ultimate Loss vs Executable Loss: dangle the big threat, execute only small ones. Executable Loss ≤30% of invested time/resources (ideally ≤15%; 2-5% usually enough); >30% → demoralized quit. Response to loss is PROPORTIONAL to what's invested (level 37 restart >> level 2 restart). Grace systems: threaten firing, exercise pep-talk/demotion level losses, then grant the second chance → gratitude (CD8 setting up CD5); but if abused, MUST execute the loss or the system loses credibility for everyone. Happiness = expectations vs circumstances (B-student depression; billionaire→millionaire suicide (Merckle); start stern then lenient, never the reverse).
- Caveat — Avoiding the Avoidance: user must know EXACTLY what to do to prevent the loss. Tetanus pamphlet study (Leventhal/Singer/Jones): fear alone ✗, plan alone ✗, fear+plan ✓. Confused = feels stupid = dismisses threat entirely ("probably not a big problem") → Status Quo Sloth wins. "The only thing we have to fear is fear by itself."
- Techniques: #46 Rightful Heritage (make them believe it's already THEIRS, then threaten removal: click-around credits "Sign up to SAVE your 3000 credits" >> "sign up to GET 3000 credits"; escalating earned-discount widget 5→10→15→20%; startup vesting cliff keeps leavers; companies wrongly give raises only at counter-offer time = rewarding undesirable actions (wife's 40% offer story); inheritance feuds). #86 Evanescent Opportunity + #65 Countdown Timer (Diablo III Treasure Goblin steals the show mid-boss-run; limited-time offers; car salesman "boss will change his mind"; charity "matching donor next hour"; countdown makes the narrowing window VISIBLE — display smallest sensible unit, usually seconds). #85 Status Quo Sloth ("I'll sign up tomorrow" = never; build the sloth INTO your Endgame via habit loops — Eyal Hook Model: Trigger→Action→Variable Reward→Investment (investment = CD4: photos, tags, folders); habitual users must SPEND energy to leave). #84 FOMO Punch (CD8 against itself: fear of losing what you COULD have had; Jobs to Sculley "sugar water or change the world" — "I knew I would wonder for the rest of my life"; Dutch Postcode Lottery: your POSTCODE wins, non-players watch neighbors party → outperformed normal lotteries; Coricelli: brain compelled to avoid even low-probability framed losses; FOMO Punch for Discovery phase, Status Quo Sloth for Endgame; gamified work systems must stay VOLUNTARY — play must be voluntary (Huizinga) — FOMO-punch employees in, don't mandate). #50 Sunk Cost Prison (quit = admit hundreds of hours became nothing → play more to feel awesome again → deeper spiral; design: make users accumulate and KNOW they're accumulating things lost on exit; Facebook = friends+photos+games hostage vs Google search = zero exit cost (one "let me try Bing" away; countered by Alfred Effect personalization); deploy only when user has an urge to leave, pair with White Hat).
- Kahneman Fourfold Pattern: low-probability loss → risk-averse (insurance); high-probability loss → risk-SEEKING (prefer 90% lose $200 over certain loss — glimmer of hope). "Fear keeps us alive, hope is what we live for."

## Ch13 Left vs Right Brain Core Drives (start)
- No Core Drive present = no action (plus hidden 9th: Sensation). CD numbers = designer shorthand; 8 CDs are MECE.
- Left Brain (logic/ownership/analysis — EXTRINSIC, goal/result-oriented): CD2, CD4, CD6. Right Brain (creativity/social/curiosity — INTRINSIC, experience/process-oriented): CD3, CD5, CD7. (Symbolic naming, not neuroscience; placed left/right on the octagon.)
- Extrinsic = task not fun, goal/reward drives it ($10k per dung dug: motivated but task still not fun; remove reward → hate it MORE than before). Intrinsic = would PAY to do it (casino: "lost $200 but so much fun" = buying the joy of maybe-winning; make the $40 outcome certain → it becomes factory labor).
- vs Pink's Drive/SDT: Pink calls Mastery intrinsic; Chou: basketball practice is extrinsic (goal-driven) — TEST: remove the goal/progress permanently — would they still do it? Social hangouts/creative puzzles pass; points/mastery-progress don't. ("World ends tomorrow" test: you'd play basketball WITH loved ones, not practice hoops.) Michael Wu: Intrinsic REWARDS ≠ Intrinsic MOTIVATION (doing it FOR any reward = extrinsic).
- Overjustification Effect: pay someone who draws for joy, then stop (or decrease to insulting $0.02) → they stop entirely; intrinsic replaced by extrinsic. Paid acceptable $20 → optimizes for COMPLETION not QUALITY (fast unrefined output).

## Ch13 Left vs Right Brain (cont.)
- Big pay hurts complex work: Ariely "Large Stakes Big Mistakes" — 5-months-pay group performed WORSE than 1-day-pay on cognitive tasks; LSE: financial incentives reduce intrinsic motivation + ethical compliance → net negative performance. Pink: rewards narrow focus — great for clear-path mechanical tasks, terrible for creative "candle problems". Ariely NYT: mechanical skill → higher pay = better; any rudimentary cognitive skill → higher pay = worse.
- Education: innate learning drive (CD7+CD3) shifts to grades/diplomas (CD2+CD4) → minimum work, cheating, forgetting after exams; overachiever senior didn't know WHY math matters ("when do people EVER use advanced math?") until given career examples; office hours only visited for grade problems; "remember 80% instead of forgetting 80% = 400% better" met with "what's the point? test's done."
- Candle Problem (Duncker; Glucksberg '62): paid group 3.5 min SLOWER (CD4 overtakes CD3); with tacks shown OUTSIDE the box (creativity removed) paid group was FASTER. Free = play = flexible mind.
- Ariely Social Norms vs Market Norms: free favors happily done (lawyers, furniture, open source); offer 1¢ → insult + relationship questioned; mother-in-law paid for Thanksgiving ≠ wine gift. Lindt truffles 10¢→5¢→1¢ demand +240%/+400% (market), 1¢→FREE → each takes only ONE, demand −50% (social norm: don't be a jerk). GIFTS keep social norms UNLESS priced aloud ("this 50¢ chocolate bar" = insult; "this chocolate bar" = eager help); gift cards & red envelopes = cash disguised as Social Treasure; dating: naming the $80 steak kills it.
- Extrinsic advantages: Discovery-phase hooks ("$100 gift card" beats "fun with friends"), focus on mundane tasks; reward must respect time value ($2 for a month of searches, $3 for weeks of tasks = worse than nothing). Michael Wu ramp: Extrinsic Rewards → attract; Intrinsic Rewards (recognition/status/access) → transition; Intrinsic Motivation → long-term.
- Making an experience more intrinsic (the chapter's action list):
  1. MORE SOCIAL: ask invites/ratings only AT First Major Win-State ("Wow this is awesome!" moment) — count the minutes to it, every second before it bleeds users; onboarding-time invite prompts DELAY the Win-State; default share text must read as true endorsement, editable, not ad copy; better still: collaborative play inside the Desired Action — Water Coolers, Group Quests.
  2. MORE UNPREDICTABILITY: controlled randomness — vary feedback dialogue, random tips; variable rewards (Mystery Box expected-trigger / Easter Egg unexpected-trigger); Eyal: "variable rewards are one of the most powerful tools to hook users"; reward is extrinsic, VARIABILITY adds intrinsic thrill; CAUTION: CD7 is Black Hat — long-term suspense on big stakes (annual raise roulette) = discomfort/churn; keep the variable-reward action SHORT and easy (slot lever, feed refresh); if the action must be long, name the possible rewards up front (Italy/France/Denmark vacation).
  3. MEANINGFUL CHOICES + FEEDBACK (White Hat intrinsic — golden corner CD3, hardest to implement): 100 users / 3+ genuinely different paths to the same Win-State; all-100-same-path = zero creativity; Lego test; avoid Google+ Problem (choice flood = decision paralysis = feel stupid = Anti-CD2): 2-3 meaningful options at any point; perception-of-choice isn't enough.
  + BOOSTERS as rewards: Megaman 1987 boss-order choice (absorb abilities → strategize optimal order); airline-miles/credit-card/coupon optimizers = CD3 making extrinsic rewards intrinsically fun (the PROCESS of strategizing engages).
- Bigger picture: hook with Left Brain, then QUICKLY transition to Right Brain (Meaningful Choices, Social Bonding, Refreshing Content #73, Variable Rewards) or face overjustification, staleness, burnout.

## Ch14 White Hat vs Black Hat (start)
- White Hat = top of octagon: CD1, CD2, CD3 (powerful, fulfilled, in control). Black Hat = bottom: CD6, CD7, CD8 (obsessed, anxious, addicted — strong but you feel not in control). CD4+CD5 = middle, neither.
- Origin: Endgame study — viral-then-dead games (obsession/urgency, grinding "labor", Sunk Cost Prison, mass exodus when a new game's Discovery marketing hits) vs timeless games (Poker, Chess, Mahjong, crosswords, Starcraft, WoW, DotA/LoL, CS): Endgame feels like wellbeing/satisfaction (instrument-playing). Naming from SEO white/black hat; brains can't patch exploits like Google can.
- White Hat's critical weakness: NO URGENCY ("I'll change the world… after a nice breakfast"). Black Hat creates the urgency ("gun to head: change the world now" — you do it, hate it, drop the cause when free). Pure White Hat loses to ambient Black Hat distractions (email, Facebook) → user procrastinates AND feels bad.
- Zynga case: mastered Black Hat ("data-driven design") → great short metrics (monetization, virality, DAU), users don't FEEL good → leave when they can; re-skins = "playing the Endgame during Onboarding"; doubled down on slots 2014; Cottle credits Candy Crush to "animations and special effects" — wrong: Candy Crush & Minecraft last via CD3; Zynga Poker & Words with Friends = accidental CD3 (Poker/Scrabble) = their only evergreens. PREDICTION RULE: no Black Hat → no breakout; no White Hat → burnout and exit.
- Black Hat ≠ unethical: voluntary self-applied Black Hat for goals you WANT (SnuzNLuz donates to a charity you HATE on snooze; Shredder Clock destroys cash). People hate Black Hat used to make them buy/obey/overwork — they still comply (it's obsessive) but burn out or revolt.
- Ethics litmus: (a) full transparency of intended purpose, (b) implicit/explicit opt-in. "Please"/"thank you" = accepted manipulation; hypnosis = ethical because transparent + opted-in. Gamification ≠ mind control: "conversions +100%" usually = 8%→16%; it moves the on-the-fence, never those with no value in the action.
- White Hat timing: workplace/long-term engagement = White Hat (meaning, mastery, autonomy ≈ SDT/Drive); corporations wrongly assume pay = obligation → employees give minimum (CD4 paycheck + CD8 job fear). Google: assumed every employee is a (would-be) entrepreneur who leaves if unhappy.

## Ch14 White Hat vs Black Hat (cont.)
- Design assumption that improves everything: "the moment the experience stops engaging, people leave" (no one HAS to play). Google's White Hat stack: CD1 mission + "Don't be evil"; CD2 eight engineer levels (+9th "Senior Google Fellow" invented for Jeff Dean); CD3 20% time; CD4 project ownership + pay; CD5 campus culture where stagnation is socially undesirable.
- Black Hat timing: sales/eCommerce/fundraising = immediate conversion (Woot = CD6+CD7). Morf Media selling to risk-averse banks: flip CD8 around — every non-compliant day = lawsuit landmines → NOT buying is the risky act. Investors = greed (CD2/4) + fear (CD8); White Hat interest alone → 6 months of "wanting to see more"; they close only when convinced they'll LOSE the deal. Chou's round: $600k target, "we're closing the round" email → angry investors piling in → forced cap raises 800k→900k→1M→$1.05M with money rejected to keep the cap credible. "With White Hat alone, people are always intending, never doing."
- RewardMe story: 11x competitor numbers, $1.5M deal, still folded (personnel/funding/legal) — product quality ≠ company success; Chou happier now on White Hat motivation vs counting runway (CD8).
- Israeli daycare ($3 late fine, Gneezy & Rustichini "A Fine is a Price"): fine REPLACED CD1/CD5 (be a good parent, don't burden staff) with a cheap market price for tardiness → MORE lateness, and removal didn't restore the social norm. Fix: loss must be threatening — $1/minute works (also adds CD6 + agency/CD3). NEVER swap White Hat for weak Black Hat.
- THE SEQUENCE (core design law): White Hat environment first (feel powerful, good opinion) → Black Hat at the conversion moment (CD6/CD8, sometimes CD5) → IMMEDIATELY back to White Hat so they feel good about what they did. Battle Camp: 8h troop boss + 50-min Torture Breaks → 7.5h in, boss at 20% → pay $10 or 25 players waste 8 hours (CD8 purchase) → then showered: achievement (CD2), trophies (CD4), teammates cheer "you're our hero!" (CD5) → "maybe $10 well spent" → trained for next purchase. TOMS shoes: post-purchase regret killed by African-child impact story (CD1) — every glance at the shoes re-affirms "I'm a decent human." Charities: photos/letters from the adopted child = feedback mechanics against donor remorse. Good Endgame design REQUIRES White Hat immersion.
- CD4 & CD5 = middle of octagon, dual-natured: ownership empowers until possessions own YOU (vintage car fear, wealth obsession, compulsive organizing); social bonds joy vs peer pressure (up to suicide over others' judgment).
- Ethics close: transparency + opt-in/out freedom, "is this the impact I want?"; Chou turned down a tobacco/beer conglomerate.

## Ch15 Other frameworks through Octalysis
- Origin of Octalysis: 100% derived from games, THEN validated with science. Games = voluntary ("tainted motivation" = work/taxes you must do), so every game = mass motivation experiment with millions of voluntary subjects. Clone studies: near-identical games where one flops — differences as small as seconds before a popup, challenge order, economy scarcity control; these subtleties beat graphics/animation/theme. Chou plays pay-to-win games while refusing to pay, noting exactly WHERE the compulsion spikes. ("can / have to / want to / compelled to / inspired to / excited to" spectrum.)
- Self-Determination Theory (Ryan/Deci; Pink adds Purpose, renames Competence→Mastery): Competence=CD2, Autonomy=CD3, Relatedness=CD5, Purpose=CD1 — all WHITE HAT (+right-brain CD5). SDT doesn't explain gambling/exclusivity (Black Hat = "non-self-determined"); perfect for employee/student long-term design, blind to the dark side.
- Bartle 4 Player Types (MUD inventor; for voluntary virtual worlds, Bartle himself says not for workplaces): Achievers = CD2+CD6 (some 3,4); Explorers = CD7 (seeds 2,3,6); Socializers = CD5 (some 3,7,4); Killers = CD2+CD5 (recognition of superiority; some 3, 8-avoid-humiliation, 4). CD1 = context any type can carry; no type is built on CD8 (pure Black Hat ≠ voluntary worlds). Amy Jo Kim's Social Action Matrix verbs: Explore/Create/Compete/Collaborate; Marczewski 6 workplace User Types — Philanthropists = CD1 (companies usually punish them!), Players = CD4.
- Lazzaro 4 Keys to Fun: Hard Fun = CD2+CD6 (fiero); Easy Fun = CD3+CD7 (clay/Legos, can't lose; randomness makes games easier/casual-friendly — dice vs chess with your kid); Serious Fun = CD1+CD4 (real-world impact/money); People Fun = CD5+some CD8 (avoid looking bad; hide-and-seek thrill).
- Flow (Csikszentmihalyi) mapped as difficulty bands, top→bottom: far-above-skill = CD8 (survival anxiety) → CD6 (challenged, frustrated-but-possible, Moat #67) → CD2 (moderate, competent — the Flow) → CD3 (heart of Flow, creativity↔accomplishment rotation) → CD4 (mindless collect/organize, relaxing or boring) → CD5 (chat room, easy bonding) → CD7 (lowest skill: gambling, movies = "relaxation" not boredom). CD1 outside the model entirely — it's WHY you're in the experience at all. Designer must raise difficulty as skill grows or users outpace it and churn.
- Fogg Behavior Model: B = Motivation + Ability + Trigger (all three or no action). Tiny Habits: 2 push-ups after urinating — goal set LOW so it never gets skipped (skipping weakens self-confidence and kills the habit; success = CD2 confidence loop). Chou's disagreement: Fogg says motivation is unpredictable, focus on ability+trigger; Octalysis puts motivation first.

## Ch15 (cont.) Fogg continued + McGonigal

Game def: "voluntary attempt to overcome unnecessary obstacles" (Suits via McGonigal). Games don't make things easy — they make them MOTIVATING. Still: most products are too hard (Anti-CD2 in Onboarding) — ease remains high priority there.
- Fogg's 6 motivators (Pleasure/Pain, Hope/Fear, Social Acceptance/Rejection) ≈ CD2,4,5,7,8 + hidden CD9 Sensation; missing CD1 (dying for a cause) and CD6-type heuristics. - McGonigal (Reality is Broken): Epic Meaning=CD1; Social Fabric=CD5; Urgent Optimism = CD2+CD6 (hope just before success + act NOW; gamer trusts designer that a win-path exists); Blissful Productivity = CD2+CD3+CD4 (deep immersion in work with immediate obvious results; WoW stamina). ## Ch17 Designing from scratch (Octalysis Prime showcase)
- Octalysis Strategy Dashboard = 5 definitions, in order:
  1. BUSINESS METRICS → Game Objective. Ranked by importance; end-metrics (signups, purchases) above enabler-metrics (views, shares) — "the enabled is more important than the enabler"; each interface optimizes ONE metric; test: "if top-3 metrics excel and the rest are modest, is the project successful?" No fluff that can't be A/B tested.
  2. USERS → Players. (Level III: separate Octalysis chart + Desired-Action track per player type.)
  3. DESIRED ACTIONS → Win-States: ALL small steps in chronological order across the 4 phases (Discovery: hear→land→click; Onboarding: skim→share→watch→about→email; Scaffolding: weekly loop habits; Endgame: premium sub, office hours, weekly technique application). Every designed element must push a Desired Action or it's a distraction — throw it away. Every committed Desired Action = a Win-State. Living document — add actions as design evolves.
  4. FEEDBACK MECHANICS → Triggers: first list the MEDIUMS you control (site, book, email, social, talks, videos, workshops), then the mechanics with their drives (countdown=6/8, unlockable content=3/6, dice overlays=4/7, tweet bird=5/7, glowing choice=2/7, videos=1/5/7, status points=2/4/6, hello bar=1/2/6/7, collection sets=2/4/6/7, leaderboards=2/5/6/8, certificates=2/4/5/6, popups=1/2/5/6/8, growth charts=2/3/4/5/6/8, exchangeable points=2-8).
  5. INCENTIVES → REWARDS: list from abundant to scarce (status points … signed book … time with founder … license). This list = your arsenal; when a design needs a reward, pick from here.
- Then the IDEATION loop: walk all 8 CDs generating ideas toward the Desired Actions; REPEAT the cycle — richer ideas come in later passes. Sample ideas per drive: CD1 believable transformation messaging, pro-bono showcases, "Good Gamification" elitism, Save-the-World Weekly; CD2 never-confused design (Glowing Choice + Desert Oasis), every login hits a delightful Win-State, Knowledge Scores→certificates, per-drive octagon profile growth ("specialists"); CD3 self-serve Octalysis tool, member submissions + Office Hours feedback, member voting on next analysis (passive observers → active players), weekly technique to apply in real projects, specialists host sessions; CD4 collection sets as KEYS to later magic, Easter-egg free signed books, Personal Octalysis Report, Chou Coins exchangeable currency; CD5 entry watercooler (FB group) + premium forum, Group Quest gate ("next level opens after X certificates" → invites), accountability buddies, top-10% random showcase (NOT a stats leaderboard), regional group Conformity Anchors, hashtag relatedness, tip-a-coin-and-it-doubles generosity design; CD6 admission question as commitment barrier (small, not procrastination-inducing), dangle locked content with exact unlock path, keep certificates genuinely hard (symbol value), Torture-Break+Anchored-Juxtaposition+Evanescent-Opportunity combo: new premium content free for 1-3 days (countdown) → locked for a year (countdown) → public; keyword collectors get 50% off; daily 1-hour group quests; CD7 unpredictable video backgrounds (people finish videos to see where he appears next), site Easter Eggs (secret footer door → Portal badge), Mystery Box dice on big Desired Actions; CD8 FOMO Punch reminders, full-refund guarantee (kills an Anti-CD), deliberately NOT designing Sunk Cost Prison (ethics), post-cancel grace access months (fights FOMO fear of subscribing).
- Advanced pattern from repeat cycles: per-drive stats + octagon profile growth; leveled drives grant themed powers (CD1 pro-bono influence, CD2 gold profile, CD3 host sessions, CD4 +30% coins, CD5 moderation/mentoring/social treasures, CD6 cheaper unlocks, CD7 extra dice rolls, CD8 grace months) → Socializers get socializer benefits, Achievers get achiever status, each plays differently; mastery of several drives = Chain Combos (#40) compounding (more rolls × more coins per win × cheaper unlocks = hyper-powered play worth pride).
- Funnel wisdom: many ideas INTO the funnel good; many ideas OUT toward implementation = lack of focus. Octalysis asks the right questions; checklist-use gives the shell without the essence. Remaining work: reward schedules, activity loops, balanced economies, wireframes.
- Magician caveat: revealing your own live game techniques to your market weakens them.
